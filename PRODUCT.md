# Word Quest — product brief

Word Quest is an offline collection of ten 2D learning games for children aged 2–11. A parent chooses an age band; a child picks a bird and a game, then learns by speaking, steering, matching, arranging, or experimenting. The web app and Capacitor Android/iOS packages use the same plain ES modules and Canvas 2D scenes. No account or network is required to play.

## The promise

- **Child:** I can understand what to do from the picture, try again safely, and feel my actions change the world. A short visit is rewarding; open-ended play has no artificial finish.
- **Parent:** Age choices genuinely change challenge, progress is durable, sound and microphone are optional, and there are no ads or purchases in the play space.
- **First release:** A working offline Android app with responsive touch, audible instructions where the device supports them, solvable levels, and a consistent, legible game shelf. iOS follows from the same source tree after native verification.

## Product boundaries

No manipulative streak pressure, random paid rewards, lives sold for money, copied game art, third-party tracking, or remote child speech storage. The existing streak is an optional progress record, never a reason to nag or lock play. Keep Tinker Town an open sandbox unless a later product decision explicitly changes it.

## Original expansion direction

Use the existing bird and world as recurring characters. Add small mechanics with one clear verb: **Feather Kitchen** (sequence ingredients to cook a narrated recipe), **Moonlight Post** (sort illustrated parcels by sound or shape), and **River Builders** (experiment with bridge pieces so animals cross). Each should begin with one playable action, then add a single twist; no copied characters, screens, level layouts, or sounds from reference products.

## Research informing this brief

- [BabyBus Play Land](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sinyee.babybus.cube.global) groups short offline activities across role play, cooking, art, and puzzles. The transferable idea is a varied, recognizable world with small sessions, not its characters or content.
- [Papo World](https://www.papoworld.com/) offers playful house, restaurant, and shape themes. The transferable idea is physical cause and effect alongside guided learning.
- [Toca Boca World](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tocaboca.tocalifeworld) shows why an open-ended space can sit beside goal-driven activities without a timer.
- [Google Play Families policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335) and [Apple's Kids guidance](https://developer.apple.com/kids/) govern any later commerce or external links. Verify current requirements before store submission.
